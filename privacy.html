Delivery Timer — Privacy Policy

Developer: Pozivaimarketing (Oleksandr Pozyvaibatko), Kassel, Germany · pozivaimarketing.de
Last updated: 15 November 2026

English

Delivery Timer does not collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline. Shift data (number of parcels, times, stopwatch laps, language choice) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you end a shift, clear app data or uninstall the app. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only permission used is vibration (feedback when you mark a parcel as delivered). Questions: contact us via pozivaimarketing.de.
